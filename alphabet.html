Learn the Sign Language Alphabet

Here are the signs for each letter of the alphabet in Indian Sign Language (ISL). Click on the images to view the details and note for each letter.

A

Description:  Make a fist with your thumb curled outside the fingers.
            Your fingers are not touching each other but curled inwards, and your thumb is placed on the outside.

B

Description: Open your hand with all your fingers extended and together. Keep your palm facing forward, with the thumb positioned against the side of the index finger.

C

Description:  Shape your hand into a “C” by curling your fingers inward while keeping your palm facing outward. Your thumb will also curve to form the letter.
                .

D

Description:  Form a "D" shape by creating a circle with your index finger and thumb, while the remaining three fingers are held straight. Keep your palm facing outward.

E

Description: Curl your fingers inward towards your palm, and make a fist with your thumb wrapped around the side of the fingers, as if you are holding something loosely.

F

Description:  Form a circle by joining the tip of your thumb and index finger, while keeping the other fingers extended together. Your palm faces inward.

G

Description: Extend your index finger and thumb apart, while keeping the rest of your fingers curled inward. Your palm should face sideways, and the hand should form the letter “G..

H

Description:   Extend your index and middle fingers together, keeping the other fingers curled down. Your palm should be facing outward.

I

Description:  Hold up only your pinky finger, keeping the other fingers folded in a fist. The palm faces outward.

J

Description:  Start by holding up your pinky finger (like for the letter "I"), and then move it in a "J" shape in the air.

K

Description:  Extend your index and middle fingers, keeping them together, while your thumb should be placed between them. The other two fingers are curled in. Your palm faces outward.

L

Description: Extend your index finger and thumb, making an "L" shape, while keeping the other fingers curled down. The palm faces outward.

M

Description:   Curl your thumb under your pinky finger, while keeping the other fingers extended and together. Your palm faces forward.

N

Description:  Place your thumb under your ring finger, while keeping the other fingers extended together. Your palm faces forward.

O

Description:  Make a circle by curling all your fingers together (including your thumb), forming an "O" shape. Your palm should face outward.

P

Description:  Extend your index and middle fingers, while keeping the other fingers curled. The thumb should rest under the index and middle fingers. The palm faces outward.

Q

Description:  Similar to the "G" gesture, but the hand should face downward, and the thumb and index finger should form the letter “Q” while the other fingers are curled inward.

R

Description: Cross your index and middle fingers over each other, while keeping the other fingers curled in. Your palm faces outward.

S

Description: Make a fist with your thumb placed over your fingers, so your hand is in a closed fist shape with the thumb wrapped around the outside of your fingers.

T

Description:  Make a fist with your thumb placed between your index and middle fingers, while the other fingers are curled in. The palm faces inward.

U

Description:  Extend your index and middle fingers together, keeping the other fingers curled in. Your palm faces outward.

V

Description:  Extend your index and middle fingers apart, forming a "V" shape. Your palm should face outward.

W

Description: Extend your index, middle, and ring fingers together, while keeping your pinky and thumb curled inward. Your palm faces outward.

X

Description:  Cross your index finger over your middle finger. The rest of the fingers are curled in, with the palm facing outward.

Y

Description:  Extend your thumb and pinky finger, keeping the other fingers curled inward. Your palm faces forward, with your hand in a "Y" shape.

Z

Description:  Hold your index finger up and trace a “Z” shape in the air, moving your hand from left to right to form the letter.
