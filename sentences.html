Learn the Sign Language Alphabet

Here are the signs for each letter of the alphabet in Indian Sign Language (ISL). Click on the images to view the details and note for each letter.

A

Description: The sign for 'A' is made by forming a fist with your hand and leaving your thumb on the outside. It’s a simple and quick gesture.

B

Description: To make the sign for 'B', extend your fingers and thumb, while keeping the hand palm facing outward.
